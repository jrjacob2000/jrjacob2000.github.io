# JRJacob Financial Application Suite (`jrjacob2000.github.io`)

Central application portal and interactive launcher for the three financial and stewardship applications created by **JRJacob (`@jrjacob2000`)**:

1. **[GraceBooks — Church Financial Tracker](https://jrjacob2000.github.io/Grace-Book/)**
   - Church financial tracking with Google Drive JSON storage, tithes and collections tracking, multi-fund accounting, annual budget dashboard, and monthly board reports.
2. **[CapitalFlow — Income, Expense & Savings Ledger](https://jrjacob2000.github.io/Capital-Flow/)**
   - Personal finance ledger for recording bi-weekly and monthly income, expenses, and cumulative savings with yearly Google Drive JSON synchronization and visual analytics.
3. **[Clan E-Fund Ledger — Collaborative Emergency & Investment Fund](https://jrjacob2000.github.io/Clan-eFund/)**
   - Collaborative family emergency fund ledger and investment tracker for member contributions, medical disbursements, and BPI fund allocations.
